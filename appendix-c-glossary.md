# Appendix C · Glossary

## A

**Adiabatic accessibility** — A later work-state *Y* is adiabatically accessible from an earlier state *X* (written *X* ≺ *Y*) if the operation can get from *X* to *Y* with the workspace left ready to work again — the only lasting trace being the work produced. The formal statement of the savepoint. §A.1

**Art-Ops-In-A-Box** — The operator as the unit of operation: a living, breathing, walking Historiotheque. The mobile, distributed form of the art operation. Ch. 09

**Audit trail** — The public, inspectable record of everything the operation did and when — the studio logs, the versioned declarations, the archived artifacts with their provenance. Anyone can check. Ch. 04

## B

**Boundary** — The marked and defended edge of the workspace. Inside the boundary is the operation; the world knocks before entering. Ch. 01

## C

**Chain, the** — The unbroken succession of sessions. The rule is simple: don't break the chain. Ch. 06

**Civic virtues** — Integrity, transparency, responsibility, truth, accountability. As properties of the record (Ch. 04) and as habits of the operator's character (Ch. 09).

**Collection** — A bounded, published unit of the operation's output: a finite group of series, deliberately assembled, with its target stated in advance. The unit at which the operation's growth becomes *legible*. Ch. 03

**Continuity, operational** — The operation staying alive: the unbroken succession of sessions, each inheriting the last and bequeathing to the next. Primordial — nothing in the operation outranks it. Ch. 06

**Cultural software** — Programs designed as cultural objects; and by extension the Historiotheque itself — an operating system for the practice, with official releases and semantic versioning. Ch. 01

## D

**Declaration of Production-Year** — The standing testimony of the operation, written each fall for the production year beginning the next October: what was done, what continues, what was dropped and why. The Master Plan in its durable form. Appendix B

**Delta (Δ)** — An appreciable change between two workspace states. Deltas *signal*: a stack that grew, a tool left out, an unfamiliar object from the field. The operator reads deltas every morning. Ch. 08, §A.2

**Dérive** — An unplanned drift through the field with the recorder of attention running; fieldwork's counterpart to the soundwalk. Ch. 03

**Distribution** — The fact that the operation lives at several addresses at once: the studio, the field, the archive, the public repositories — one Historiotheque, distributed over time and space. Ch. 08

## E

**Edification** — The moral formation of the operator: what kind of witness to be, now that you know what to do. Moral, not religious. Ch. 09

**Error (as data)** — A mistake honestly logged: what was done, what happened, what it cost, what changes. Errors struck through and annotated, never rewritten, never deleted. The error log is the operation's senior partner. Ch. 05

**Examen** — The operator's honest self-audit against the seven-item checklist of the mature Art Operation, conducted in the log. Ch. 09

**Experiment** — A bounded, hypothesized, cheap, logged trial, run in designated space — never inside a production series mid-run. Ch. 07

## F

**Field, The** — Everywhere the operation collects: the street, the mountain, the library, the conversation. Fieldwork gathers raw material; the Lab manufactures it. Ch. 03

**Field kit** — The portable minimum of the operation — notebook, recorder, tools of capture — packed and ready at all times. Ch. 08

**First Witness** — The Witness-Box's defining role: the primary, unmediated, truthful record of the work as it happened. Ch. 01

**Fluid intelligence** — The raw capacity to absorb the unfamiliar and learn fast. It atrophies without use and rebuilds with it; the operator rebuilds it deliberately by studying outside the discipline. Ch. 07

## H

**Historiotheque** — The art operation's house: the studio, sound-design laboratory, and research office as one integrated cultural-software system — and, in its mobile form, the operator's own body of practice, wherever it goes. Spelling: French-style *-thèque*, from Greek *-theca*. Ch. 01

**Historiotopia** — The three-dimensional surface of the workspace: the tables, walls, stacks, and screens in space. At any moment it is in a state; the state is subject to change. Ch. 08, §A.1

**Historiotopic function** — *h(t)*: the function carrying the workspace from one configuration to the next over time. Its deltas are the changes of *h*; its derivative is the historiotopic velocity. §A.2

**Historiotopic velocity** — How fast the workspace's configuration is changing. A series in full production has high velocity; a stalled operation has velocity near zero — the formal statement of momentum death. §A.2

**H-house / H-op** — The operating house / the operation, in the warehouse-logistics formalization. The H-house interrupts the flow of inputs from outside to inside and outputs from inside to outside. §A.3

## I

**Implementation** — Putting everything learned into practice, session after session. The operation *is* the implementation; the manual is inert until executed. Ch. 05

**InFlow / OutFlow** — The flows the H-house interrupts: inputs (materials, data, signals) from outside to inside; outputs (works, publications, records) from inside to outside. Consume the InFlow; produce the OutFlow. §A.3

## L

**Lab, The** — Where the operation manufactures: the studio, the sound-design environment, the digital workbench. Fieldwork collects; the Lab transforms. Ch. 03

**Logistics of the Historiotheque** — The discipline of InFlow and OutFlow managed as deliberately as the work itself. An operation that manufactures brilliantly but cannot receive, store, or ship is a warehouse with no doors. §A.3

## M

**Master Plan** — The standing plan the operation follows: the series in production and its definition, the collection target, the year's arc, the publication commitments. One page, visible every morning; amended between series, never abandoned mid-series. Ch. 05

**Minimum session** — The smallest unit of work that keeps the chain alive on the worst day: twenty minutes, one log entry, one small physical act. A minimum session is a successful act of continuity. Ch. 06

**Momentum death** — The stalling of a series not by decision but by drift — velocity near zero. Restarting costs the reconstruction of the momentum continuity would have preserved for free. Ch. 06

**Mutation** — A function *M* carrying workspace *A* to *A′ = M(A)*; the change *A′ − A* is the Δ-workspace. §A.2

## O

**Official Release** — The versioned publication of the Historiotheque as cultural software (semantic versioning), issued when the operation relaunches after a shutdown. Appendix B

## P

**Population** — A generation of many artifacts from one procedure, designed as a study rather than a masterpiece. The operation generates populations and selects from them; the artist does not wait for inspiration. Ch. 03

**Precedence (≺)** — The ordering relation of work-states: *w₁* ≺ *w₂* means *w₁* precedes *w₂* — the workspace actually moved from one to the other, through real operations. §A.1

**Preparation** — The six standing preparations of the operation: workspace, self, raw materials, reference materials, consulted archives, prepared publication. Ch. 02

**Production year** — The operation's year: October to October, matching the Declarations, which are written each fall for the year beginning the next October. Appendix B

## R

**Redundancy** — Multiple copies of everything, so that no single loss can destroy the operation's memory. Archival collapse engineered against. Ch. 02

**Refcards-System** — The operation's reference backbone: one concept per card, numbered RC-YYYY-NNNN, cross-referenced across the whole practice — atoms of knowledge in a local card catalog. Ch. 02

**Rehearsal** — Production with the pressure removed: technique exercised for its own sake, scheduled explicitly, so it is there when the series needs it. Ch. 07

## S

**Savepoint** — The state in which a session ends: the next step written down, materials staged, the log closed, the workspace surveyed. Four parts, ten minutes, the highest-leverage time in the operation. Ch. 06

**Series** — The bounded unit of production: a finite, named run of related artifacts sharing one definition, one technique, one question. Ch. 03

**Soundwalk** — A listening expedition through the field with the recorder running; the primary instrument of field collection in the sound practice. Ch. 03

**Studio log** — The session-by-session written record of the operation: what was done, what was decided, what failed. The operation's memory. Ch. 04

**Survey, morning** — The ritual reading of the workspace before touching anything: stand at the boundary, ask what changed, notice the deltas. A velocity reading of *h(t)*. Ch. 08

**Switchboard Method** — The daily rhythm of the operation: alternating across projects in short intervals, the operator carrying no plan between them — only the next move — with logging and archiving automatic. Ch. 03

## T

**Tax on forced backtracking** — The compounding cost of reconstructing what continuity would have preserved for free: re-learning what was known, re-finding what was had, re-deciding what was decided. Ch. 06

**Trace loss** — The loss of materials, files, or records — pieces of the operation's memory that do not regenerate. One of the enemies of continuity. Ch. 06

## V

**Verifiability Stack** — The operation's documentation apparatus, documented: the system making the work identifiable, timestamped, citable, and findable — the apparatus that substitutes for the institutional imprimatur. Ch. 04

## W

**Witness-Box** — The box that testifies: the workspace — and the operator — as the primary, truthful record of the work. First Witness of Truth. Ch. 01

## Z

**Zero** — A day the operation stopped. A *recorded* zero — written down with its reason — is a fact the operation can work with. A *silent* zero is the beginning of amnesia. Never let a zero go unlogged. Ch. 06
